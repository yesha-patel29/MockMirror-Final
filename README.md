🎯 MockMirror – AI-Powered Interactive Interview Platform

**MECIA HACKS 3.0**

MockMirror is an AI-powered interactive mock interview platform designed to help candidates prepare for real-world interviews through realistic interview simulations, intelligent question generation, and personalized performance analysis.

It provides an interactive interview environment where candidates can practice answering questions, improve their communication skills, evaluate their technical knowledge, and identify areas for improvement.

Instead of simply asking predefined questions, MockMirror combines AI-driven interview interactions with speech recognition and facial behavior analysis to provide a more comprehensive interview preparation experience.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. AI-Powered Mock Interviews

- Generates interview questions based on the selected role and interview context.
- Supports technical and non-technical interview preparation.
- Provides an interactive interview experience.
- Uses AI to support dynamic interview interactions and follow-up questions.

### 2. Resume Upload and Information Extraction

- Upload resumes in PDF format.
- Extracts relevant information from the uploaded resume.
- Automatically populates candidate details using extracted information.
- Reduces the need for manually entering candidate information.

### 3. Resume and Job Description Analysis

- Supports resume and job description-based preparation.
- Helps identify relevant skills and potential skill gaps.
- Provides insights to help candidates understand their preparation requirements.

### 4. Intelligent Interview System

- Uses AI to understand interview context and candidate responses.
- Supports adaptive follow-up questions.
- Adjusts interview interactions according to the candidate's responses and interview context.

### 5. AI-Based Answer Evaluation

- Evaluates candidate responses according to the interview questions.
- Provides AI-generated feedback on individual answers.
- Generates suggested answers to help candidates understand how to improve their responses.
- Helps identify correct concepts, missing information, and areas requiring improvement.

### 6. Speech Recognition and Communication Analysis

- Uses speech-to-text technology to capture spoken responses.
- Analyzes communication-related aspects of interview performance.
- Provides insights into speaking speed and filler-word usage.

### 7. Facial Behavior Analysis

- Uses computer vision to analyze facial behavior during interviews.
- Examines aspects such as face visibility, positioning, head movement, and looking away.
- Provides additional behavioral insights as part of interview evaluation.

### 8. Final Interview Report

- Generates a report after interview completion.
- Displays interview questions and candidate responses.
- Includes AI-suggested answers and personalized feedback.
- Presents performance insights and improvement areas.

### 9. Open Library Fact Checker

- Includes a Fact Checker component to support information verification during interview preparation.

## ⚙️ How MockMirror Works

The platform follows an interactive interview preparation workflow:

1. **Candidate Setup:** The candidate provides the required information and uploads their resume.
2. **Resume Processing:** Relevant information is extracted from the uploaded resume.
3. **Interview Configuration:** The candidate selects the required interview context and settings.
4. **AI Interview:** The platform conducts an interactive mock interview using AI-generated questions.
5. **Response Analysis:** Candidate responses are processed and evaluated.
6. **Communication and Facial Analysis:** Speech and facial behavior are analyzed during the interview.
7. **Performance Evaluation:** AI-generated feedback and suggested answers are prepared.
8. **Final Report:** The candidate receives an interview performance report with feedback and improvement insights.

---

## 🧠 Project Architecture

MockMirror combines multiple components to create an interactive interview preparation experience:

- **AI Interview Engine:** Handles AI-powered interview interactions and question generation.
- **Intelligence Layer:** Supports intelligent processing of interview context and candidate responses.
- **Speech Recognition Layer:** Captures spoken responses using browser-based speech recognition.
- **Facial Analysis Layer:** Uses MediaPipe for facial behavior analysis.
- **Evaluation Layer:** Processes candidate answers and generates feedback.
- **Reporting Layer:** Organizes interview results and generates the final assessment report.
- **Fact Checker:** Provides an additional information-verification component.
